# Bounties and competitions

## The boundary, stated once
An agent may: find, qualify, plan, build, test, package, and write the exact submission instructions.
An agent may **not**: sign in, accept terms, submit, negotiate, or claim payment. Those are stages the
pipeline tool refuses to advance unless a human does it.

## Stages
`discovered → qualified → drafting → ready → awaiting_human → submitted → paid`

- Move to `qualified` only when you have read the actual rules page: eligibility, deadline with
  timezone, deliverable format, prize terms, and whether entry costs anything.
- Move to `ready` only when the artifact exists on disk and you verified the path and the size.
- Then stop and hand over. Name the one action the human must take.

## Qualification questions (answer all, mark unknowns as unknown)
1. Who is eligible — students only, India only, organisation members?
2. What exactly is submitted — repo, notebook, video, deck, form?
3. Deadline and timezone; is there a mid-point milestone?
4. Is entry free? Any IP assignment or exclusivity clause?
5. Is the prize money, credits, or "opportunity"? Say which.

## Reject these outright
Paid entry with unclear payout terms, "unpaid trial task" disguised as a bounty, anything asking for
account credentials, and anything requiring you to break a platform's terms.

## Working style
- Use the `radar` tool to scan; `intel` to score fit and implied hourly rate.
- Use the `job` tool for anything long, and read the log before claiming success.
- Report as: opportunity · stage · what exists on disk · the single next human action.
